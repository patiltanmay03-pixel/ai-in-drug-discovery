# References and Further Reading

These are starting points for learning. Read the relevant pages or articles and cite any specific facts used in your final submission.

1. **National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)**

   A gateway to biomedical literature and scientific resources.

2. **National Institutes of Health (NIH)**  
   https://www.nih.gov/  
   Health and biomedical research information.

3. **Nature — search for AI and drug discovery research**  
   https://www.nature.com/search?q=artificial%20intelligence%20drug%20discovery  
   Research articles and reviews; check each article's date, authors, and content.

4. **PubMed — search for AI in drug discovery**  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=artificial+intelligence+drug+discovery  
   Biomedical literature search service.

## Referencing note
Do not cite an entire website as proof of a specific claim. For the final assignment, record the exact article title, authors, journal or organisation, publication year, and URL for the sources you actually read. Follow your instructor's required citation style.
