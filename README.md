## 1. Introduction
Drug discovery is the process of identifying and developing potential medicines. It often involves studying biological targets, screening chemical compounds, laboratory experiments, and clinical trials. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning can help researchers analyse large datasets and prioritise compounds for further investigation.

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional drug discovery can require substantial time, resources, and experimental work. Researchers are exploring computational methods that may help organise data and prioritise promising candidates earlier in the research process.

## 3. Objectives
- Understand how AI can support drug discovery.
- Describe common uses of machine learning in pharmaceutical research.
- Identify benefits and limitations of AI-based methods.
- Demonstrate a simple, fictional compound-ranking example using Python.

## 4. How AI Can Help
1. **Data analysis:** Finds patterns in chemical and biological datasets.
2. **Virtual screening:** Helps prioritise compounds for further study.
3. **Property prediction:** Estimates selected properties when suitable data and validated models are available.
4. **Research planning:** Helps researchers decide which candidates may deserve additional experiments.

## 5. Methodology
- Review introductory scientific and institutional sources.
- Summarise key applications of AI in drug discovery.
- Run the educational Python demo in `demo.py`.
- Compare the demo's fictional scores and discuss why they are not medical or scientific predictions.

## 6. Educational Demo
The included script uses made-up compounds and made-up feature values to demonstrate ranking by a simple weighted score. It is **not a trained AI model**, does not use real drug data, and must not be used to choose or recommend medicines.

## 7. Benefits
- Can help analyse large datasets.
- May reduce the number of candidates requiring early-stage screening.
- Can support prioritisation and hypothesis generation.

## 8. Limitations
- Results depend on data quality and model design.
- A computational prediction does not prove that a compound is safe or effective.
- Laboratory experiments and clinical trials remain essential.
- Bias, uncertainty, reproducibility, and interpretability must be considered.

## 9. Expected Outcome
This project should help a beginner explain the potential role of AI in drug discovery, identify its limitations, and understand a basic computational ranking workflow.

## 10. Conclusion
AI can support parts of drug discovery by analysing data and helping researchers prioritise compounds. However, AI outputs are only one part of a much larger scientific process. Experimental validation and appropriate safety testing are necessary before any medicine can be considered for use.
