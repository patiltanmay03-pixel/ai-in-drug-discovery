# ai-in-drug-discovery
A beginner-friendly bioengineering project exploring AI in drug discovery.
AI in Drug Discovery

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important tool in biotechnology and bioengineering. It can help researchers study biological data and identify potential drug candidates.

Objectives

- To understand the role of AI in drug discovery.
- To learn how AI can analyse biological and chemical data
- - To explore how technology may support medical research.
- To understand the benefits and limitations of AI in healthcare.

How AI Helps in Drug Discovery

1. Data Analysis: AI can analyse large amounts of biological and chemical data.
2. Drug Screening: AI can help researchers identify promising compounds for further study.
3. 3. Prediction: Machine learning models can predict certain properties of drug candidates.
4. Research Support: AI can help researchers prioritise experiments and save time.

Applications

- Biotechnology
- Pharmaceutical research
- Computational biology
- Personalised medicine

Advantages

- Faster analysis of research data.
- Helps researchers screen many compounds.
- Supports data-driven scientific decisions.

Limitations

- Predictions depend on data quality.
- Results require scientific validation.
- AI cannot replace laboratory testing or clinical trials.

Conclusion

AI has the potential to improve parts of the drug discovery process. Combining AI with biotechnology and laboratory research may support the development of new medicines.
